Pre-Launch Preparation

Before you dive in, figure out three things: what playstyle to pursue, how much time to invest, and whether you'll spend money. These three decisions shape your first 72 hours — and whether you'll still be playing a month from now.

For your first save, don't chase perfection. Push the main story past the first major milestone, get comfortable with the combat rhythm and resource loop, then decide whether to restart with a "perfectly planned" save.

Early-Game Resource Planning

Resources are scarce early on. There's only one core principle for prioritization: get your main team fully built first, then worry about bench depth and collection. Use the table below to allocate your early-game resources:

Resource TypeEarly-Game UsePriority
XP MaterialsFunnel into 1 main DPS + 1 supportHighest
General CurrencyOnly buy stamina / key dungeon ticketsHigh
Pull CurrencySave until pity, don't spend on scattered pullsHigh
Cosmetic ItemsDon't touch early onLow
Library Master's Tip: Hold off on all "limited-time packs." During the launch period, 90% of packs offer worse value than simply saving for pity.

Common Pitfalls

The three biggest traps for new players: leveling all characters equally, clicking every notification dot, and spending pull currency on the standard banner. Remember, every decision during the launch period should revolve around one metric: how fast your main team comes online.

  • Pitfall 1: Equal-opportunity leveling — resources spread thin, no one hits hard.
  • Pitfall 2: Compulsively clearing notification dots — stamina wasted on junk dungeons.
  • Pitfall 3: Going all-in on the standard banner — left with nothing when a limited character gets a rate-up.
