Troubleshooting a Friend's Sprout's Computer Issues

Work Log FF14 Computer Repair Updated 2022-09-01 · Sect Master

藏
A troubleshooting log for a friend getting only 20 FPS in FF14: Task Manager showed 100% CPU usage but the frequency was only 1.99GHz (base 2.5GHz). Remote diagnosis revealed the laptop wasn't plugged in, causing CPU throttling.

Conclusion First

100% CPU usage caused the FPS drop

This is a case-specific diagnosis and does not apply to all situations.

How It Started

Late at night when I was about to go to sleep, someone in the gaming group — a sprout (newbie) — was complaining that their computer only got 20 FPS in FF14. We were all wondering what kind of specs would produce only 20 FPS in this game. Then the sprout posted screenshots from Task Manager:

3. By All Rights, It Shouldn't Only Get 20 FPS

Looking at the sprout's screenshots, there's one thing to notice:

CPU usage is 80%, but the frequency is only 1.99GHz. As is well known, the i5-7300HQ has a base frequency of 2.5GHz, so there are only two possible causes:

  • CPU thermal throttling
  • Power plan locking the frequency

Remote Diagnosis

By remotely accessing the sprout's computer, I found out it was not plugged into power. After plugging in the power adapter, the frequency returned to normal. Then I launched the game — only about 40 FPS. I opened Task Manager and saw the CPU was maxed out — found the cause.

The next step was to check the game's CPU usage and how it's being used, then adjust the corresponding strategy accordingly.

Final solution: Reinstall the game, play in fullscreen mode, or get a new computer.
